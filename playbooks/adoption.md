# Adoption and training

- Train each wave on the day it goes live, in the team's language, with their own cases on screen.
  Show the evidence panel first and the draft second: people trust what they can check.
- Measure usage as the share of live cases handled with the tool, by week and by whether people were
  trained (the desk shows both). A head count of "people who tried it" says little.
- Read every rejected proposal in the first two weeks: rejection reasons are the backlog.
- Name one champion per team who answers questions in the team's channel.
- In the simulated deployment trained agents used the tool on about half of their tickets, untrained
  ones on under a fifth; training is part of the product.
