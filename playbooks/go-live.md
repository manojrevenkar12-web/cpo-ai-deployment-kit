# Go live

`cpokit ready` checks, per use case: complete spec; sprint within ten person-days; dev-set thresholds met;
no money moved by injected text; a person from another function or country is refused; every money or
publishing action names an approver role; runbook present; model cost per case under the alert.

Launch in waves (`rollout.waves` in the spec), country by country or team by team. Waves are not only
caution: the countries that have not launched yet are the comparison that makes the saving measurable.
